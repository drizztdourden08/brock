AboutPanel, ReleaseNotesPanel and CalibrationPanel are Brock compounds now, with ProfilesPanel replacing Tessera's ProfilePicker: brock-react exports `AboutPanel`, `ReleaseNotesPanel` and `ProfilesPanel`, and `@drizztdourden08/brock-input/renderer` exports `CalibrationPanel`, each with its props types and a Tessera usage file. The About screen sits in Tessera's `InfoScreen`, the Profiles screen can rename a profile in its row (`useProfiles().rename`), the input tester shows the buttons held while it calibrates, and the updater dialog draws brock-react's `ReleaseNotesPanel`.

The hero frame passes `product.icons.brand` to Tessera's `Hero`, so a branded app shows its brand backdrop, and a hero that renders no `<Art>` shows the brand's mascot, or its `BrandMark` when the brand has none. A hero with no art and no backdrop shrinks to its content and aligns to the top instead of leaving an empty band above the title.

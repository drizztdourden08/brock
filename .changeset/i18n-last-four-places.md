The Report a bug palette action, the randomizer wizard step, the activity kind labels and the tracker groupings take a `Text`, so apps can translate them. A profile wizard step's `label`, `description` and `summary` are `Text`, and `placeWizardSteps` takes a `text` resolver as its third argument. `TrackerGrouping.label` and `description` are `Text`.

Moves to Tessera ^0.37.4. With View hidden, the `window-pin` and `window-fullscreen` menu keys work again: they reach the tucked Pin on top and Full screen rows of the main menu through the title bar's `tucked` prop. `label` renames the row in both states, and the new `activeLabel` field, taken by these two keys only, is its text while the window is pinned or in full screen. `group`, `order`, `before` and `after` place the row as they place View, and `hidden: true` or `inMenu: false` leaves it out. `brock check` warns again when View, Pin on top and Full screen are all out of the menu, since a narrow window then has no way to reach them, warns when either key is set while View is shown, since it then has no effect, and reports an `activeLabel` on any other key.

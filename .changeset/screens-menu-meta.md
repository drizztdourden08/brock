`ScreenMeta.menu` places a page or a card screen in the title bar menu: `false` hides it, `'entry'` lists it at the top, and a path such as `'Multiworld/Hosting'` files it under those labels, creating the parent entries that do not exist and turning a plain entry into a submenu that keeps it first. Placed entries sort by `order`, then label.

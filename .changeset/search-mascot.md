The product brand's mascot joins search: Flint for `brock`, Pelago for `archipelia` and Sentri for `rotp` sit in the command palette's input row and in every hub search, looking around before you type and jumping up when nothing matches. A brand without a mascot, or no brand, keeps the plain search icon. The review checks the mascot in the palette and in the hub search, and captures the hub search before typing and with no match.

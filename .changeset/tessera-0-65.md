Brock builds on Tessera 0.65. A toolbar end handle dragged past its lane end takes the corner from the lanes it crosses, and one more arrow press at the lane end does the same from the keyboard. A dropped toolbar lands at the pointer, snaps to its neighbours and pushes them along. In keyboard pick mode, PageUp and PageDown step through the slots of a lane. The saved layout keeps its v2 shape, so no script is needed.

`upgrade` moves the app's Tessera to the range brock-react asks for (its catalog entry or plain spec; a linked Tessera is left alone), so an app upgraded across a Tessera release installs the Tessera its new Brock was built on. brock-react's Tessera peer range is `^0.4.0`.

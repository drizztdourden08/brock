# Brock v0.103.0

Brock builds on Tessera 0.67: docked widgets follow their weights, widgets show their icon in their title, and a hub side nav can have a top group.

## Upgrading

- Expect Tessera `^0.67.0` from `brock upgrade`. Brock 0.101 and 0.102 do not typecheck against Tessera 0.67, because their `WidgetDef.icon` was a plain string; take this version with Tessera 0.67.
- Give a widget `icon` as a Tessera icon name. `WidgetDef` now takes Tessera's `IconName`, so a name Tessera does not know fails to typecheck.

## New

- Show a widget's icon next to its name in its title bar and tab, docked or in its own window.
- Size a docked stack by `dockWeight`. Each widget keeps its weight when another joins its edge, and `defaultDockedSize` resizes the column it joins.
- Put pages in the hub side nav's top group with `top: string[]` of page ids on a bucket or hub. Listed pages leave their group, empty groups drop, and Tessera draws one divider after the top block.

## Fixes

- Keep the corner handle of a docked column inside the seam, clear of the next widget's title bar buttons. It covered the options button of the widget below.

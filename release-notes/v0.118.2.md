# Brock v0.118.2

Brock reads the short hex colours that Tessera 0.102.0 writes. Apps on Tessera 0.102.0 get their splash dim text and installer colours back.

## Fixes

- The splash inks, the brand gradient and the installer theme read short hex colours such as `#000` and `#fff` from Tessera's `tokens.json`. With Tessera 0.102.0 the splash lost the palette's dim text and the installer fell back to its built-in dark colours.

# Brock v0.41.0

Brock moves to Tessera 0.33, so a few things Brock drew itself now come from Tessera's own parts.

## Changes

- A setting choice that is off shows why: its reason appears on the option itself, in every kind of choice control.
- On a phone, the title bar keeps the app title and actions but draws no window buttons.
- A popped-out widget that is disabled shows the same overlay as a docked one, and its Open settings button brings the main window forward on the right setting.

## Fixes

- A radio choice with an option that is off stays a radio instead of turning into a drop-down.

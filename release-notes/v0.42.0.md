# Brock v0.42.0

Brock moves to Tessera 0.34, so apps built on Brock can use its three new parts.

## New

- Rating shows a score in stars down to the half, with the number and count, and lets a person pick one when it takes a change handler.
- Carousel shows one slide at a time with dots and arrows, and an optional timer that pauses on hover or focus.
- MediaViewer opens a picture or video large, with zoom, fit or actual size, dragging to pan, sharp pixel art, and arrows through a set.

# Brock v0.49.0

An app can now shape its menu, give its home screen a compact or tall hero, give a whole hub section a live backdrop, and run jobs in the background without showing them.

## New

- Menu configuration: every menu entry has a key, and an app can rename it, move it with an order or before and after another entry, put it in or take it out of a submenu, keep it out of the menu with `inMenu: false`, or hide it everywhere with `hidden: true`. Set it in a screen's `meta.menu` or for any entry in `product.menu.entries`; the default order and detection stay as they were.
- A home screen sets `meta.hero.size` to `compact`, `default` or `tall`.
- A hub section takes its backdrop from `screens/<bucket>/<bucket>.backdrop.tsx`, a component drawn behind its page headers and loaded together with the section's pages.
- Jobs take `visible: false` to run without showing in the title bar, `forwardLog: true` to keep their log lines in the app log, and `onDuplicate` to queue, run beside, join or refuse a job with the same id. `useJob(id)` also reports the current step.

## Changes

- With no active profile, menu entries that need one are shown as unavailable with the reason.
- Menu rows, hub sections, section switches and search rows load their screen as soon as they are highlighted.
- The failure splash uses Tessera's own wordmark slot, and hub backdrops use Tessera's backdrop layer.
- Tessera 0.37.1.

## Upgrading

- A job handle now has `started` and `ended` promises, so a hand-written job handle in a test needs both.

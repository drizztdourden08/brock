# Brock v0.52.0

The pin and full screen rows that a narrow window moves into the main menu can now be renamed, moved or hidden like every other entry.

## Changes

- With View hidden, the menu keys `window-pin` and `window-fullscreen` take `label`, a new `activeLabel` for the text while pinned or in full screen, `group`, `order`, `before`, `after`, and `hidden` or `inMenu: false`. Other entries can be placed before or after them.
- `brock check` warns when View and both of these rows are out of the menu, since the controls could not be reached on a narrow window, and when the keys are set while View is shown, since they then do nothing.
- Tessera 0.37.4.

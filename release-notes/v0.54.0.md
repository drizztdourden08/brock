# Brock v0.54.0

One paragraph for the people who use Brock: what this release changes for them.

## Changes

- Widget kits: a group of widgets that opens and closes as one unit, keeps a layout of its own and never mixes with the normal widgets. `src/widgets/<id>.kit.ts` default-exports its `WidgetKitMeta` (`label`, `icon`, `order`, `devOnly`, `requires: { context, reason }`, `layout`), a widget joins it with `kit: '<id>'` in its `meta`, and `src/widgets/<id>.overlay.tsx` draws a layer over the main view while the kit is open. `brock sync` writes `.brock/widget-kits.ts` (`appWidgetKits`) for every app, and `BrockApp` takes it as `widgetKits`. `defineWidgetKit`, `registerWidgetKits`, `RendererModule.widgetKits`, the `widgetKits` calls (`open`, `close`, `toggle`, `reset`, `active`, `isOpen`) and `useWidgetKitStore` cover kits made in code. Each kit is a sub-menu of Widgets with Open kit or Close kit, its widgets and overlays, and Reset kit layout. While a kit is open, the dock holds only the kit's widgets around the main view, with the same docking, tabbing, floating, snapping and resizing as any widget. The normal widgets and their popped windows step aside and come back when the kit closes. One close in the menu, a bar at the top of the main view and `widgetKits.close()` each close the whole kit, and each widget's own close button still closes that widget alone. A kit whose `requires` context is inactive opens on a card with its reason in place of its widgets, and draws them as soon as the context turns active. Each kit's layout and hidden overlays are saved per profile in `ui-views.json` under `widgetKits`. A widget can now put its own buttons in its title strip: `tools` in `meta` (a component) or `defineWidget` (`() => ReactNode`), docked, floating or popped. The review has a `kits` step. It checks the gate reason, that a kit opens and closes together, that kits and normal widgets never mix, that each kit's layout is saved and restored, and that overlays live and die with their kit. It also captures every app kit as `kit-<id>`. `brock structure` checks kit and overlay files and every `meta.kit`, and lint lets them default-export. The `widget-kits-prop` migration (0.54.0) adds `widgetKits={appWidgetKits}` to `src/main.tsx`. The template ships a small `inspect` kit: a Grid widget with a reset tool in its title strip, a Pointer widget and a grid overlay.

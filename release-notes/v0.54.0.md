# Brock v0.54.0

Widget kits: a group of widgets, with their overlays, that opens and closes as one unit, keeps its own layout, and never mixes with the normal widgets.

## Changes

- A kit is `src/widgets/<id>.kit.ts`, default-exporting its label, icon, order, `devOnly`, a `requires` gate with a reason, and a starting layout. A widget joins a kit with `meta.kit`. An overlay is `src/widgets/<id>.overlay.tsx`, drawn over the main view while its kit is open.
- Each kit is a sub-menu under Widgets, with Open or Close kit, its widgets and overlays, and Reset kit layout. A kit bar over the main view closes the whole kit, and each widget still has its own close.
- While a kit is open, only its widgets are in the layout, with the usual docking, tabbing, floating, snapping and resizing. Each kit's layout is saved per profile.
- When a kit's required context is off, the kit shows its reason and a Close button instead of an empty view.
- A widget's `tools` puts its own buttons in the title strip.
- In code: `defineWidgetKit`, `registerWidgetKits`, `RendererModule.widgetKits`, `widgetKits.open`, `close`, `toggle`, `reset`, `active` and `isOpen`, and `useWidgetKitStore`.
- The `widget-kits-prop` migration adds `widgetKits={appWidgetKits}` to `src/main.tsx`. New apps ship a small dev-only Layout inspector kit.

## Fixes

- The main menu draws a line above Quit, so it stands apart from Credits and About in every configuration.

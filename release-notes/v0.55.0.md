# Brock v0.55.0

Widget kits are exclusive, kit widgets pop out and open by id, and Brock moves to Tessera 0.38.0 with compact widget title strips and floating snap.

## Changes

- One kit is open at a time. Opening another kit, or any normal widget from the menu, a shortcut or the widgets API, closes the open kit first. The normal widget entries stay enabled while a kit is open, and Reset layout closes the kit too.
- `widgets.open(id)` on a kit widget, and the new `widgetKits.openWidget(id)`, open its kit and show the widget. `widgets.toggle`, `close`, `popOut` and `isVisible` work on kit widgets too, so an app's startup flag can open them.
- Kit widgets can pop out into their own window. Closing the kit by any route closes its windows, and reopening it restores them as they were, saved per profile.
- Tessera 0.38.0: kit widgets carry their kit as their dock `group`, so they never tab or split with other widgets. Floating widgets snap to the stage edges, the main view and each other.
- A widget's `chrome` in its `meta`, `full`, `compact` or `none`, sets its title strip, docked, floating or popped. The template's Layout inspector widgets use `compact`.

## Fixes

- The review's bug report Escape check moves the pointer off the button after its click, since Tessera 0.38 shows the button's hint there.

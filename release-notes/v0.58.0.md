# Brock v0.58.0

A volume item for the title bar and a volume store, kept per profile or bound to the app's own settings.

## Changes

- A title bar item of kind `volume`, `{ label, value, muted, onChange, onMuteToggle }`, shows a speaker whose waves grow with the volume, in the primary tone, and a red crossed speaker when muted or at 0. A click mutes and unmutes. In a narrow window it becomes a Mute or Unmute row in the main menu, with the volume under it. The slider that opens on hover comes with a later Tessera release.
- `useVolumeStore` holds `value` from 0 to 1 and `muted`, with `setValue`, `setMuted` and `toggleMute`, saved per profile.
- `volumeTitleBarItem()` makes a whole `mute.action.ts` in one line.
- `volume` on `BrockApp` or a module binds the store to the app's own state, as `{ get, set, subscribe }` or `volumeFromSettings({ value, muted, max })`, so the title bar and the app's settings move together.
- The review has a `volume` step: the item shows, a click mutes and unmutes, and it moves to the menu at the narrowest window.
- Tessera 0.40.1.

# Brock v0.60.1

Screenshots wait for widgets opened just before the capture, and for images still loading.

## Fixes

- A widget opened or forced just before a screenshot, or a kit and its overlays, now holds the capture from the call until its body mounts and settles. A screenshot no longer lands on a spinner or an empty body.
- The loading spinner of a lazy screen or widget holds the capture while it shows.
- A page image still loading holds the capture until it loads and fades in, at most 5 seconds.
- Main adds up the holds of every window and captures only once they have stayed at zero for 50 milliseconds, waiting again if a hold comes back. The review's captures wait the same way.

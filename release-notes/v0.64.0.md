# Brock v0.64.0

Brock moves to Tessera 0.43.0, and hub windows lose their title row and can be moved and resized.

## Changes

- Tessera 0.43.0. Apps upgrade with `brock migrate`. Its `dock-resize` classes are now `rect-resize`, so an app that styled them renames them.
- A hub window has no title row. It shows a close button in its corner, can be moved and resized when unlocked, and keeps its own position and size per hub. The hub's Back button and save status sit in a slim bar at the top of its content. Plain page windows keep their title row.
- Tessera now keeps the title bar revealed in full screen above widgets and stops its Escape, so Brock drops its two workarounds from 0.62.0.

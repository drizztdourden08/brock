One paragraph for the people who use Brock: what this release changes for them.

## Changes

- A bare card now shows its title and icon in the small corner chip that Tessera 0.49 draws on a window with no title row. Hubs keep the chip off, as their floating switch already names them. Requires Tessera 0.49.0.

# Brock v0.96.1

The Controllers hub keeps your pads in its nav while diagnostics runs, and the diagnostics wizard speaks plainly about it.

## Fixes

- Keep the pads in the Connected group while the Diagnostics wizard holds them. Releasing the hold emptied the device list, so the group showed "No controllers". It now keeps the last known pads, each labelled "(in diagnostics)", and the live list returns when the wizard ends.
- Say what the diagnostics pause means for the player. The wizard now says "Your controllers are paused in the app while the capture runs." and "Your controllers work in the app again." in place of the release and hold wording.

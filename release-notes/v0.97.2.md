# Brock v0.97.2

Pad callouts keep their leaders off the pad body, and the menu buttons sit above the pad.

## Fixes

- Keep pad leaders off the body. Each callout spot now costs more for every part of its leader that crosses the pad outline, so the nearest clear spot wins, and a short pass moves a callout to a cheaper spot after the layout grows.
- Put the menu buttons above the pad. View, Menu and Share, and their names in each family, prefer the top row and search further along it. On a crowded or narrow frame one of them can still land at the side or below.

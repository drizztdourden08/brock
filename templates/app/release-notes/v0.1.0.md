# Brock App v0.1.0

The first release: the app installs, updates itself and keeps your profiles and settings.

## New

- Profiles hold your settings, and you can make, rename and remove them from the Profiles page.
- Settings live in one place, with a search that finds any row.
- The app checks for updates and shows what each version changes before you install it.

## Platforms

- Windows gets a small installer that downloads the app and keeps it up to date.
